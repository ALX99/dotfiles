# Investigation

Read-only unless edits are separately authorized.

1. Identify the question and relevant entry points, data flow, consumers, and tests. For motivation, inspect history and documentation rather than inventing intent.
2. Verify uncertain claims where practical. For suspected causes, state predictions and choose a safe experiment that distinguishes alternatives; reduce the case without changing the failure.
3. Answer with paths and evidence. Separate observed behavior, intended contracts, and inference; explain disagreements and material unknowns. Give tradeoffs when a decision is requested.
